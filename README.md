# Next Move Planner

A single-page calculator for planning a UK house move: selling your current home, buying the next one, and comparing mortgage deals.

- Enter salaries, savings, your current home's sale price and remaining mortgage, and a target purchase price.
- Choose whether to set the deposit or the amount of savings to keep back; the other is worked out for you.
- See loan to value, stamp duty (England and NI home-mover rates), borrowing limit and monthly payments.
- Add mortgage deals and compare them by true cost per year over the fixed period.

Everything runs in the browser. Figures and deals are saved in your browser's local storage only and never leave your device.

Live at https://ed-barnes937.github.io/next-move-planner/
